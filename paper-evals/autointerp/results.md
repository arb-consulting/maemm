## Fire fraction as covariate

`fire_fraction` is the share of ALL 64 of the MAEMM's rollouts on which the target feature exceeds the SAE gate somewhere, as stored by `sae_self` -- NOT the share among the 16 rollouts the M arm happens to show, which is near 1 by construction (they are the top 16 by activation) and therefore degenerate as a covariate. The hard stratum is exactly the set the MAEMM never fires on, so this is the covariate that should separate it.
